# Contributing

Suggestions, corrections and news that something now exists are all welcome as issues or pull requests.

The full guide is [docs/contributing.md](docs/contributing.md), also published on the [site](https://ohtf.github.io/awesome-missing-nhs-things/contributing/). It covers what belongs on the list, how to format an entry, when to write a brief, and how to preview and check your changes.

In short:

- Each section of the list is a page in [`docs/list/`](docs/list/). Start every entry with one status marker: `missing`, `restricted`, `partial`, `building` or `solved`.
- Cite a source for every claim about what exists, who runs it, how it is licensed or what it costs.
- Sign off your commits under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://openhealthtechnology.org/community/dco/) with `git commit -s`, and never include real patient data.
