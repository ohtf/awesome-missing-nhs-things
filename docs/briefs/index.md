# Briefs

A brief is the long version of an entry: what the gap is, what exists today, what would close it, and the sources for each claim. Entries link to their brief, and each brief links back to its section of the list.

- [Open clinical instruments, delivered as code](open-clinical-instruments.md): why scored questionnaires and rating scales, including the ADHD scales, are so rarely available as reusable open code, and what would change that.
- [Medicine shortages at the point of prescribing](medicine-shortages-at-the-point-of-prescribing.md): the national shortage data exists, but prescribers don't see it when they choose a drug.
